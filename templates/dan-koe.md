# dan-koe

## about
A minimal line-art idea reel: one idea, principle or mental model (a habit, a way of working or thinking, a lesson about life or creative work) told in short, calm lines by one quiet narrator. Every line gets one simple line drawing that grows out of a single white dot on pure black and makes that line's idea visible (a tree that splits in two at every step, steps a dot climbs, orbits around a centre, a spiral, a grid of dots that fills, a path); one small lowercase line of text sits at the top. It goes from a quiet hook, through the common belief and the turn, to how the principle works, and ends on one quiet payoff line as the main drawing completes and its tips light up. For ideas, principles, mental models, habits, focus, learning, creativity, work and life lessons. Not for machines and mechanisms, history, news, products, places, data comparisons or character stories.

## look
- This style is deliberately sparse and overrides several base rules; where they disagree, this section wins:
  - The ground is PURE BLACK everywhere: flat, with no glow, gradient, vignette, light pool, texture or shapes (overrides "backgrounds are calm but never flat" and "avoid pure black and pure white").
  - Text has no backing at all: no panel, box, stroke or shadow; the black ground is its contrast (overrides "text always sits on a contrasting backing"). One text size, 44 px (overrides "headlines 72 to 140 px").
  - Drawings are hairlines and solid dots only: no filled shapes except dots, no 3-value shading, no highlights, no gradients, no contact shadows, no outlines around shapes, no glow and no ctx.shadowBlur (overrides the base assets section; ignore its glossy capsule example entirely).
  - No charts, graphs (h.graph), routes (h.route), maps, icons, arrows, labels, panels or diagrams: a number or a change over time is shown by the drawing itself (more branches, more lit dots, a higher step).
  - Large black areas are part of the style: the band between the top line and the drawing stays empty on purpose.
- Colour is a set of relations in OKLCH with chroma 0 everywhere: there is no hue at all, whatever the topic.
  - ground: lightness 0 (pure black), the whole frame.
  - line (a drawing while it grows): lightness about 0.96.
  - dim (a drawing once it has completed, and lines that are not the focus): lightness about 0.60, which is the line colour at 50% opacity on black.
  - soft (secondary rings and ellipses): lightness about 0.80.
  - dot: lightness 1.0 (pure white). Dots are always the brightest thing on screen and never dim.
  - text: lightness about 0.98.
  - specks: white at 15 to 45% opacity.
  - Palette names: bg (ground), ink (dot), line, dim, soft, text. palette.accent = dim (the engine colours its caption bar with it, so it stays quiet); palette.accent2 = line.
- Line weights: 1 to 3.2 px, thickest at a drawing's start (its root, base or centre) and thinner each level away from it (width = Math.max(1, 3.2 - level * 0.28)); rings and orbits 2 px; the completion pulse ring 3 px. ctx.lineCap = 'round'. Never thicker than 4 px.
- Dots: solid white discs, radius 2 to 31 px; a drawing's start dot is 16 px.
- Type: kit.font uses "Segoe UI" at weight 400 ALWAYS, whatever weight is asked: font = (size, weight) => `400 ${size}px "Segoe UI", Arial`, so the engine's own caption stays plain too. All on-screen text is lowercase and ends with a full stop: no capitals, no bold, no exclamation marks, no emojis.
- Backgrounds (kit.bg): exactly two, named exactly "void" and "void_specks" (with the underscore), both with "plain": true in the plan's style:
  - "void": fill the whole frame pure black. Nothing else.
  - "void_specks": pure black plus 30 specks: const r = h.rng(5); for each speck x = r() * 1080, y = ((r() * 1920 - lt * 6) % 1920 + 1920) % 1920, radius 1.5 + r() * 1.5, opacity 0.15 + 0.25 * (0.5 + 0.5 * Math.sin(lt * 0.7 + r() * 6.28)), white. Nothing else.
- kit.title(ctx, text, x, y, {size = 44, align = 'center', color, k}): text only, kit.font(size), textBaseline 'middle', fill palette.text; no box, stroke or shadow. A number k fades it in over 0.25 s (opacity only, no movement); a missing k shows it at once.
- kit.panel exists only because the kit contract needs it: a 1.5 px dim hairline rectangle, no fill. Shots never call it.
- kit.post draws nothing (an empty function): pure black must stay pure black.
- Canvas helpers, exactly as the engine defines them: h.rng(seed) returns a FUNCTION (const r = h.rng(7); then r(), r(), r() give numbers from 0 to 1); never use h.rng(seed) itself as a number. h.rr(x, y, w, hgt, r) and h.poly([[x, y], ...]) take NO ctx argument and only build a path. h.asset('id', x, y, scale, params) takes NO ctx either (its first argument is the asset id string).

## motion
- Calm and procedural, never bouncy: a drawing grows at a steady beat, level after level, each piece drawing itself out of the previous one with easeOut (cubic). No anticipation and no overshoot (overrides the base motion rule), except the start dot's pop and a climbing dot's hops; no motion lines, blur trails, speedlines or bursts. The camera is locked; no parallax.
- Every shot is one small complete cycle, timed to its line:
  1. the start dot pops in at the drawing's start point in the shot's first 0.2 s (radius 0 to 16, easeOut);
  2. the drawing grows from the line's first word to 70% of the shot: t0 = sh.lines[0].start + 0.15, T = 0.7 * sh.dur, grow = h.clamp((lt - t0) / (T - t0)), passed to the asset;
  3. at T it completes: lit = h.clamp((lt - T) / 0.6), passed to the asset: its dots flash to about twice their size and settle back within 0.5 s, and its lines dim from line to dim over 0.6 s while the dots stay white;
  4. after T it holds its shape to the end of the shot (about 1.5 s, the payoff of every line readable) but never freezes: the engine rejects a shot whose frames at 55% and 80% look the same, and a still frame reads as frozen. Using p.t, each motif keeps one gentle motion: branching sways (the sway param) and its last-level tip dots twinkle (radius times 1 + 0.35 * Math.sin(p.t * 3 + i)); orbit dots keep travelling; the steps' dot bobs 6 px on its top step; the spiral's tip dot keeps circling the outer turn; the grid's lit dots breathe (radius 7 + 1.5 * Math.sin(p.t * 2.5 + i * 0.7)); the path sends a small dot along the finished line every 2 s.
- Why the timing matters: the engine rejects a shot whose frames at 55% and 80% of its length look the same, and hairlines change very few pixels. So the drawing is still growing at 55% and has completed (flash, dim) by 80%. Never let a drawing finish early and sit still.
- The completion accent, in the turn and payoff shots only, drawn by the shot (not by the asset), with q = lt - T from T on:
  - a pulse ring: a 3 px white circle around the drawing's start point, radius 20 + h.easeOut(q / 0.8) * 260, opacity h.clamp(1 - q / 0.8) * 0.8;
  - 40 sparks: const r = h.rng(11); for each spark a = r() * 6.283, d = 90 + r() * 240, radius r() < 0.3 ? 5 : 3, drawn as a white dot at (cx + Math.cos(a) * d * 1.7 * e, cy + Math.sin(a) * d * e - q * 40) with e = h.easeOut(q / 0.9), opacity h.clamp(q * 6) * (1 - 0.6 * h.clamp((q - 0.5) / 0.8)); (cx, cy) is the middle of the drawing (for the branching tree its crown, at 540, 760). They settle at 40% and keep drifting up slowly.
- The top line is shown from the shot's first frame (no entrance) and stays all shot. In the payoff shot it swaps from the setup phrase to the payoff phrase in ONE frame at T (no fade, no movement).

## plan
- Structure: 7 or 8 shots, ONE line each, one beat each, in this order (build takes 2 shots; proof is optional): with 7 or 8 shots each line stays short (about 9 to 12 words); fewer shots make every line long and heavy.
  1. hook: a quiet, surprising statement or question about the idea, in plain words. Line shapes: "Most people [common habit]. That is why [quiet consequence]." or "What if [the usual goal] is the wrong goal?" Screen: the hero motif in its simplest form (branching depth 6, 3 steps, 1 orbit, 3 spiral turns, a small grid) grows out of one dot.
  2. belief: what most people believe or do by default, and why it quietly fails. Screen: the second motif showing the default's limit (a path that wanders and loops back, steps that stay low, an orbit that circles without growing, a grid where only a few dots light up).
  3. turn: "But [the reframe]." The idea in one plain sentence. Screen: the hero doing what the idea says (splitting, climbing, filling, circling closer), with the completion accent (pulse ring and sparks).
  4. build (1 or 2 shots): how the principle works, one step per line: "Each [small unit] [does one small thing]. Then [the next builds on it]." Screen: the hero bigger and fuller than before (more levels, more steps, more orbits, more turns, more dots).
  5. proof (0 or 1 shot): one concrete everyday case, or one real number said in words, that shows the principle at work. Screen: the second or third motif showing the same pattern from another side.
  6. payoff: one quiet line that lands the principle, the shortest line of the reel (6 to 9 words, ONE thought: never a second clause joined with "so" or "and"; give the words it saves to the build and proof lines), shaped like "[The principle], one [small unit] at a time." Screen: the hero at its fullest (branching depth 9 or 10, 6 or 7 steps, 3 orbits, 7 spiral turns, a full grid), the top line swapping from its setup phrase to its payoff phrase the moment the drawing completes, with the completion accent; held to the end.
- The idea and its words come from the brief. Never quote real people, use their names, catchphrases or slogans, or the words of the reference.
- Pick the motifs whose SHAPE is the idea (from the menu in the assets section): something that multiplies, spreads or branches -> branching; slow, steady progress, levels, stages -> steps; focus, a centre, a routine, pull -> orbit; coming back around at a higher level, iteration -> spiral; many small units adding up (days, reps, attempts, pages) -> grid; the direct way against detours -> path. Choose 2 or 3: the hero (the core idea; used in the hook, turn, build and payoff) first, then one or two for the belief and the proof.
- The drawing says what ITS line says, through its params: a line about something adding up to nothing shows it (a grid with only 2 or 3 lit dots, grow stopping at 0.05; steps that stop at the second step; a path that wanders and loops back); a line about growth shows the full motif. Never show a full, successful drawing on a line about failure or waiting.
- Every shot's drawing is visibly different from every other shot's: use 3 motifs when there are 7 or 8 shots, and when a motif returns it returns changed (more steps, a different grid fill, another path form, a deeper tree), never as a copy of an earlier shot.
- Every shot idea states: the background ("void"; "void_specks" for the payoff), the ONE motif with its params (for example "branching, depth 7, seed 4, path -1: grows from its dot from the line's first word to 70% of the shot, then lit"), the exact top line: 2 to 5 CONSECUTIVE words cut out of that shot's spoken line, letter for letter (no other word, no changed word form: if the line says "remain", the top line says "remain", never "stay"), lowercase, ending with a full stop, at most 26 characters; write the spoken line first, then cut the top line out of it (the engine rejects any on-screen word the shot's line does not say). For the payoff, both phrases (the setup phrase, then the payoff phrase) are cut out of the payoff's line the same way.
- Lines: calm, plain and personal (second person, present tense), short sentences, words a twelve-year-old knows; one thought per line; no hype, no exclamation marks, no lists, no jargon. Each line adds something new. Numbers are rare: at most one real number, said in ONE line only (the hook or the proof), written as words; no later line repeats it.
- Voice: one narrator, brian (deep American narrator), unhurried, low and warm, with a pause between sentences. Tone per line from calm, quietly, slow, warm, serious: the turn serious or slow, the payoff quietly or slow; never the same tone on every line.
- Music: feel Calming Mysterious, 70-90 bpm (the reference is silent: a slow, sparse pulse felt under the voice, never a still pad); payoff_shot is the payoff beat. Sound effects: one soft pop when the payoff's drawing completes (sfx on line 0, at "end", offset -0.4); nothing else.
- Assets: exactly the 2 or 3 chosen motifs, kind "icon", each description written from its recipe in the assets section (its shape, counts, sizes and params), with its finished drawing as the FIRST state (grow 1, lit 0), then a half-grown state (grow 0.5) and a completed state (grow 1, lit 1). The shots draw the pulse ring, the sparks and the top line.

## assets
- Every motif is a line drawing with nothing behind it (no backing, card or glow): hairlines and solid white dots only, in the look section's weights and colours. It grows out of ONE start dot. Params shared by every motif:
  - grow (0 to 1): how much of the drawing exists; 0 = only the start dot.
  - lit (0 to 1, the 0.6 s after the drawing completes): dots flash (radius times 1 + 1.4 * Math.exp(-3 * lit) * Math.min(1, lit * 8.4)) and lines dim (line colour at opacity 1 - 0.5 * h.easeOut(lit)); dots never dim.
  - The start dot pops in with p.t: radius R * h.easeOut(p.t / 0.2), R = the motif's start-dot radius (16 unless its recipe says otherwise).
  - Whatever its count params (depth, n, rings, turns, cols, rows), the finished drawing fills its box: counts change how dense it is, never how big (the engine rejects an asset whose first state fills less than 55% of its box).
- Keep the four corners of the box empty (the engine rejects an asset that paints 3 or 4 of its corners) and never draw outside the box. No pulse ring and no sparks inside the asset (the shot draws them). No text. Randomness only from an h.rng(seed) created inside drawAsset.
- The menu (box sizes are final: the shots place every motif at scale 1):
  - branching (box 960 x 920, start dot at (0, 460), the box's bottom centre): a tree of hairlines that splits in two at every step. Build the segments in every call: const r = h.rng(p.seed); a recursive function branch(x, y, angle, len, level, parent) pushes a segment {x, y, angle, len, level, parent} and, unless level is depth - 1, calls itself for side -1 then side 1 from the segment's end with angle + side * (0.27 + r() * 0.17) + (r() - 0.5) * 0.1 and len * (0.72 + r() * 0.06); start with branch(0, 460, -Math.PI / 2, 228, 0, null). Growth: u = p.grow * depth; a segment of level d is drawn to k = h.easeOut(h.clamp((u - d) / 1.08)) of its length, starting from its parent's CURRENT end, so children ride the growing tips. Width Math.max(1, 3.2 - d * 0.28). A dot rides every tip that is still growing and every last-level tip: radius Math.max(2.2, 16 * Math.pow(0.74, d)). Params: grow, lit, depth (6 to 10, default 9: 2^(depth - 1) tips), seed (changes the shape, not the size), sway (radians: each segment's angle += sway * d * 0.35, so the tips move most), path (-1 = off, or the number of a last-level tip counted left to right in creation order: that tip's segment and all its parents stay at full line colour, width + 0.8, with a 10 px dot at the tip, while every other segment drops to 22% opacity). Fit, every call, after building the segments: take the smallest and largest x and the smallest y of every segment's end at full growth, f = Math.min(1, 820 / (maxX - minX), 860 / (460 - minY)), and draw every point at (x * f, 460 + (y - 460) * f): the finished crown is then never wider than 820 px (76% of the frame) at any depth, and a deeper tree is denser, never wider. At depth 9 it is a broad dome about 820 px wide.
  - steps (box 900 x 960): n (3 to 7) steps rising left to right and a solid dot (radius 31) that climbs them; this dot is its start dot and pops in with grow (below), not with p.t. Step k (0 to n - 1): an open hairline outline (left side, top, right side; 2.6 px; no fill), width bw = Math.min(116, 551 / n), centred at x = -300 + k * 560 / (n - 1), top at y = 300 - k * 460 / (n - 1), its sides running down to y 440. Growth: s = p.grow * n; step k rises from y 440 to its top while s goes from k to k + 0.4 (easeOut); the dot pops onto step 0 (scale 0 to 2.1 to 1 while s goes from 0 to 0.4), then hops from step k - 1 to step k while s goes from k + 0.4 to k + 1: x moves linearly, y follows an arc peaking 120 px above the higher top; squashed (x 1.18, y 0.78) just before take-off, stretched (x 0.9, y 1.12) in the air, squashed (x 1.22, y 0.74) on landing, then settling with easeBack. The dot's centre sits 31 px above its step's top. lit: around the dot on the top step a 2 px dashed circle (radius 140, dash [5, 9]) draws itself clockwise from the top and turns slowly (angle + 0.7 * lit), a soft 2 px ellipse (rx 200, ry 46) draws itself around the dot, and the steps dim to 32% opacity. Params: grow, lit, n.
  - orbit (box 760 x 760, start dot at the centre, radius 28): a centre dot and 1 to 3 hairline circles around it (2 px; the outermost always radius 330, circle j of rings at radius 330 - 90 * (rings - 1 - j), so 330; or 240 and 330; or 150, 240 and 330; the innermost dashed [5, 9] when there are 2 or 3), circle j drawing itself clockwise from the top while grow goes from j / rings to (j + 1) / rings, each with its own dot (radius 14, 11 or 9, smaller outward) travelling on it at its own speed (angle = p.t * (0.9 - 0.25 * j) + j * 2) and leaving a fading trail (a 3 px arc 60 degrees long behind it, opacity 0.6 down to 0). Params: grow, lit, rings (1 to 3). animated: true.
  - spiral (box 820 x 820, start dot at the centre): an Archimedean spiral hairline (2 px) whose outer end is always at radius 370: radius = 20 + 350 * theta / (turns * 2 * Math.PI) for theta from 0 to turns * 2 * Math.PI, drawn up to grow of its length (as a polyline of small steps), with a dot (radius 12) riding its tip. Params: grow, lit, turns (3 to 7).
  - grid (box 860 x 860): cols x rows small dots in a block centred on the box, spacing 660 / (Math.max(cols, rows) - 1), so its outermost dots are 330 px from the centre on the longer side (and the box corners stay empty). An unlit dot is a 1.5 px dim ring of radius 5; the first Math.round(grow * cols * rows) dots in reading order are lit: solid white, radius 7, each popping from radius 0 to 7 as it lights. Params: grow, lit, cols, rows (2 to 20 each; a real count only when the line says that number), mark (-1 or the index of one dot drawn as a 14 px dot inside a 26 px hairline ring).
  - path (box 900 x 900): from a start dot at (-380, 400) to an end ring (radius 22, 2 px) at (380, -400). form "direct": one straight hairline; "wander": one smooth curve through 7 points from h.rng(p.seed) that drifts sideways and loops back on itself twice before it arrives; "both": the direct line dim and the wandering one bright. The line is 3 px, drawn up to grow of its length, with a dot (radius 16) riding its tip and small dots (radius 5) left at every turn. Params: grow, lit, form, seed.

## shots
- The same layout in every shot:
  - Top line: kit.title(ctx, text, 540, 262, {size: 44}): one lowercase line, centred, ending with a full stop. It is the only text the shot draws (the engine shows the spoken line as a plain lowercase caption at y about 1600, no box).
  - The drawing: ONE motif asset at scale 1: branching at (540, 940) (its root on y 1400); steps at (540, 940); orbit, spiral and grid at (540, 900); path at (540, 920). It spans 60 to 85% of the frame width between y about 480 and 1400.
  - Empty black between the top line and the drawing, and below y 1420 (the engine's plain caption sits at y 1540 to 1660).
- Draw order: kit.bg, the motif, the pulse ring and sparks (turn and payoff only), the top line. Nothing else: never two motifs at once, never a second text line, never extra dots, rings, frames or shapes (overrides the base three-layer and depth rules: one flat drawing on black).
- Every shot starts fresh from its motif's start dot (the cut resets to one dot) and runs one full cycle: the dot pops in, the drawing grows to 70% of the shot, completes, holds.
- The params in every frame, one formula: const t0 = sh.lines[0].start + 0.15, T = 0.7 * sh.dur, grow = h.clamp((lt - t0) / (T - t0)), lit = h.clamp((lt - T) / 0.6), dt = Math.max(0, lt - T); for branching also sway = Math.sin(dt * 2.4) * 0.045 * Math.exp(-dt * 0.6) * h.clamp(dt * 3). Pass the motif's other params (depth, seed, path, n, rings, turns, cols, rows, mark, form) exactly as the shot idea gives them.
- The payoff shot: the top line shows the setup phrase until T and the payoff phrase from T on (a one-frame swap); the pulse ring and sparks start at T.
- Never use kit.panel, h.graph, h.route, h.speedlines, h.burst or h.stick in this style.

## director
- Flag: any colour or hue; a background that is not pure black (a glow, gradient, vignette, texture or shapes); filled shapes other than dots; lines thicker than 4 px; boxes, panels, cards, labels, arrows, icons, charts or graphs; more than one line of on-screen text, or text that is not lowercase, is bold, boxed, stroked or larger than 50 px; two drawings in one frame; a drawing smaller than 55% of the frame width or off-centre; a drawing that stops growing before the middle of its shot, or a frame where nothing moves; a payoff whose top line does not swap when its drawing completes; a motif whose shape does not match its line's idea; hype words, exclamation marks, lists or jargon in the lines; a quote, name or catchphrase of a real person; anything important below y 1420.
- Check every claim about how people, habits, learning or work behave, and any number, against standard knowledge; cut a doubtful claim rather than keep it.
