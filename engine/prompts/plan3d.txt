THIS IS A 3D REEL. The engine renders a real 3D world (three.js): it builds every character from a "look3d", every room from a "room" spec, and all furniture and small props from a built-in library. Nobody draws characters or rooms by hand. Add these fields to the JSON:

- In "style.backgrounds", every background (a real place of the story) also gets "room":
  {"upper": colour of the upper wall, "lower": colour of the wainscot under the dado rail, "floor": "wood" | "tile" | "carpet", "floor_colour": optional colour, "rug": optional colour (a rug in the middle of the floor), "lamp": colour of the hanging lamp's shade, "ceiling": optional colour, "stripes": true or false (wallpaper stripes on the upper wall), "props": up to 6 things on or against the BACK WALL, left to right, from: door, window, clock, shelf, calendar, frame, poster, cabinet, shelf_unit, lockers, plant, board, tv, counter_back, fridge, sink}
  Colours are palette names (preferred) or hex. Furniture that people stand at, sit on or put things on (counter, desk, table, chair, sofa) is NOT a room prop: the shots place it.
- Every asset of kind "character" also gets "look3d" (all ball-headed cartoon people: a huge sphere head about half their height, a short tube body, stick arms and legs ending in balls):
  {"skin": optional colour, "shirt": colour, "jacket": optional colour (a suit or coat over the shirt), "tie": optional colour, "cap": optional colour (a baseball cap) or null, "hair": "none" | "short" | "long" | "bun" | "puffs" (puffs = two grey side puffs on a bald head), "hair_colour": colour, "glasses": true or false, "moustache": true or false, "beard": true or false, "stubble": true or false, "shoes": optional colour, "scale": 0.85 to 1.15 (height), "roles": optional {"<role>": { the same fields, overriding }}}
  The supporting character plays every other person through "roles" (for example "clerk", "boss", "buyer", "courier"): each role changes the cap, hair, glasses, moustache and clothes so the people read apart. Give the main character one outfit colour and a cap or hair in another palette family. "w" and "h" are not used for characters (use 500 and 900).
- At most ONE asset that is not a character: the one object the story turns on, when the built-in props do not cover it (built-in: counter, desk, table, chair, stool, sofa, crate, plant, sign, computer, laptop, phone, paper, papers, mug, pizza_box, pizza, coins, cash, box, bag, bin). Give it "size": [width, height, depth] in metres. Usually there is none: use only the two characters.
- Shot ideas describe STAGING in the room: where each character stands or sits (left or right, near the back wall or near the camera, behind or in front of the counter or desk), whom they face, their pose and expression on each line, which built-in props stand where (on the counter, in a hand), and the camera setups in order (wide, medium, close, ots, two, insert) with the line or word each starts on.
- Leave "carry" out: 3D shots are staged in their room or set, not by screen positions.
