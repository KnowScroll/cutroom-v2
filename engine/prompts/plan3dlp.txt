THIS IS A LOW-POLY 3D REEL (PS1 look). The engine renders a real 3D world (three.js) at 270 x 480 pixels and blows it up 4x with hard pixels: flat-shaded models of a few hundred triangles, 64 px textures painted by code, vertices snapped to the pixel grid, dithered colour. It builds the host from a "look3d" and every set from a "set" spec; the subject(s) are built from low-poly parts by a modeller who follows your description; nobody draws in 2D except the header, the one label and captions. Add these fields to the JSON:

- "style.palette": name the colours the sets, host and subjects use. Always include: "sky" and "sky2" (top and bottom of the sky or studio wall), "ground", "wood", "leaf", "metal", "ink" (near-black outline of text), "text" (white), "accent" (the header number and labels, a warm yellow), "edge" (the bright edges on a cut face, orange), "cut" (the dark cut face, deep violet), plus the subject's own colours. Hex values.
- "style.font": "Nunito".
- "style.backgrounds": 2 or 3, each {"name": "<short_snake_case id; each shot's "background" is exactly one of these names>", "description": "...", "set": {...}}, where "set" is
  {"kind": "outdoor" | "studio" | "room", ...}
  outdoor: {"kind": "outdoor", "sky": [top colour, bottom colour], "ground": grass colour, "props": any of fence, pines, house, bushes, bins, hills, "house": wall colour, "roof": colour, "hills": colour}
  studio: {"kind": "studio", "wall": top colour, "wall2": colour lower down, "ground": floor colour} (a plain coloured backdrop and floor: the subject alone, big)
  room: {"kind": "room", "wall": colour, "wallpaper": "speckle" | "stripes" | "planks" | "bricks" | "tiles", "wall2": second wallpaper colour, "floor": colour, "floor_tex": "planks" | "tiles" | "speckle", "props": any of window, door, plant, shelf, sofa, rug, lamp, frame, clock, "view": colour seen through the window}
  Colours are palette names (preferred) or hex.
- "assets": FIRST the host, kind "character", "w": 500, "h": 900, with "look3d" (a low-poly animal in a suit, about 1.3 m tall, built by the engine):
  {"ears": "pointy" | "round" | "long" | "none", "snout": "short" | "long" | "beak" | "flat", "tail": "none" | "bushy" | "thin" | "stub", "stripe": true or false (a dark stripe down the forehead and on the tail), "fur": colour, "fur2": muzzle colour, "mask": colour of a patch around the eyes or null, "eye": iris colour, "beak": colour (snout "beak" only), "jacket": colour, "shirt": colour, "tie": colour or null, "tie2": the tie's stripe colour, "trousers": colour, "shoes": colour, "hands": mitten colour, "scale": 0.9 to 1.1}
  Its "description" says which animal it is and its outfit in one sentence. Its "params" and "states" may be empty.
- THEN one or two subjects (kind "object"): the real thing the reel explains, as a low-poly model the shots slice open. Each has:
  "size": [width, height, depth] in metres, at the size it will stand next to the host (1.3 m tall): 0.8 to 3 m at its largest. A small real thing is shown as a big model (a heart 1.6 m tall); a huge one as a small model.
  "parts": 2 to 6 short real names of the PHYSICAL parts inside it (nouns you could point at: a coil, a valve, a speaker, a chamber; never a process such as filtering or demodulation), in the order the script explains them (the shots slice it open and label exactly one of these per shot).
  "description": 60 to 110 words: the outer shell's silhouette in simple shapes with real proportions (boxes, cylinders, cones, a lathe profile, a pipe), its colours (palette names), what the casing is made of (texture: speckle, metal, grille, fins, planks, bricks, rock ...); then each inside part: its shape, colour (clearly different from its neighbours and from the casing), and where it sits inside (left or right, top or bottom, front or back, in fractions of the size).
- Shot ideas describe STAGING in 3D: the set; where the subject stands (x, z, and y if it hangs on a wall) and how big; where the host stands beside it (never inside it); the camera per sentence (wide, host, face, subject, part); the host's pose and expression for EVERY sentence (a different pose each sentence); when the slice opens (on which word) and which ONE inside part gets the label, with its 1 to 3 word text; the step header text.
- Leave "carry" out: 3D shots are staged in their room or set, not by screen positions.
