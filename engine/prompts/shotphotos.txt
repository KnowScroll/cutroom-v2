REAL EVIDENCE HELPERS (this reel pins real photos and source quotes beside its drawings):
- h.photo(id, x, y, w, hgt, o): draws the real photo cover-cropped into the box whose TOP-LEFT corner is (x, y), in a paper frame with a drop shadow, and its credit line in small print under it (about 40 px per line, 1 or 2 lines). o: {k: seconds since it appeared (lt - h.cue('word')): it rises and fades in over 0.45 s, a negative k hides it, leave k out to simply show it; grey: 1 for black and white; zoom: slow push-in (default 0.06); fit: 'contain' to show the whole photo; credit: false only when the same photo stays on screen from the shot before}. Returns {x0, y0, x1, y1, bottom, shown} every frame, also before it appears (shown false then; bottom = below its credit line): draw a leader line from the photo to the drawn part it shows only while shown is true. At least 360 x 200 px (420 to 560 wide is right); your own text never sits on the photo or its credit line. Size the box near the photo's aspect (w / hgt = its aspect_w_over_h) so little is cropped. Use only the photo ids listed; a photo is never stretched or redrawn by hand.
- h.quote(id, x, y, w, o): a paper clipping of the source text, TOP-LEFT at (x, y), w wide (500 to 960): the marked words dark and underlined (the underline draws itself over 1 s), the rest faded, the source name in small print under it. o: {k: seconds since it appeared, size: text px (default 32), lines: at most this many lines (default 3)}. Returns {x0, y0, x1, y1, shown} every frame. About 3 lines of 32 px text are 170 px tall plus 50 px for the source line.
- h.note(lines, source): the frame's small print, drawn last: up to 2 short notes at the bottom-left of the picture (y 1400 to 1440), e.g. 'Cut-away view, illustration', 'Not to scale', 'Time compressed', and optionally the source of the shot's facts under the picture (y 1512), e.g. 'Wikipedia: [article]'. Notes say honestly what kind of picture this is; never put facts or numbers in them.
- These three draw their own small print: never draw credits, sources or notes yourself, and keep your own labels clear of them (the photo's credit line takes the 90 px under the photo).
