# Dexter: The Wrong Serial Killer
**Genre:** Dark Comedy  
**Duration:** 60 seconds

## Scene 1 — The Discovery (0–12s)

*Dexter stands in his apartment, staring at a wall covered in photographs.*

**Dexter (inner monologue):** "I've been tracking this monster for three weeks. Twelve victims. Same pattern. Same location."

*He circles a photograph with a red marker.*

**Dexter:** "Tonight, he meets his end."

## Scene 2 — The Kidnapping (12–25s)

*A man wakes up strapped to Dexter's plastic-covered table.*

**Brian:** "WHAT THE HELL?! WHO ARE YOU?!"

**Dexter:** "I know what you've done, Brian."

**Brian:** "WHAT DID I DO?!"

**Dexter:** "Twelve people. All disappeared after visiting your establishment."

**Brian:** "BRO, I'M AN ESCAPE ROOM OWNER!"

## Scene 3 — The Realization (25–45s)

*Dexter freezes.*

**Dexter (inner monologue):** "An escape room?"

**Dexter:** "Then explain the blood on your floor."

**Brian:** "IT'S FAKE BLOOD!"

**Dexter:** "And the screaming?"

**Brian:** "IT'S A HORROR-THEMED ESCAPE ROOM!"

*Dexter slowly looks down at his knife.*

**Brian:** "WAIT… ARE YOU ACTUALLY A SERIAL KILLER?!"

**Dexter:** "Technically, yes."

## Scene 4 — The Twist (45–60s)

*Awkward silence.*

**Brian:** "Can you at least untie me?"

**Dexter:** "Sure."

*Dexter struggles with the knots.*

**Dexter:** "Huh. It's stuck."

**Brian:** "YOU'RE KIDDING."

**Dexter (inner monologue):** "For the first time in my life…"

*Dexter pulls harder. Nothing.*

**Dexter:** "…I needed an escape room expert."

*Cut to black.*

**Brian (offscreen):** "THAT'LL BE $49.99!"

# Character Appearances

**Dexter Morgan**
- Short brown hair, clean-shaven
- Olive-green fitted Henley shirt
- Dark trousers and black gloves
- Calm, emotionless expression
- Awkward when confused

**Brian — Escape Room Owner**
- Messy brown hair, slight beard
- Blue polo shirt with a small escape-room logo
- Beige trousers and white sneakers
- Very expressive, constantly panicking

# Visual Style

Dark crime-thriller atmosphere inspired by Dexter. Dramatic close-ups, intense internal narration, dim lighting, and awkward comedic pauses. The humor comes from the characters treating an absurd misunderstanding completely seriously.
