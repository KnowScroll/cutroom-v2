## Rendering (seen)

- **rendering style**: flat 2D cartoon on a real geographic map (countryball map story): real coastlines, countries as flat fills (the focus country saturated orange, other land pale beige, highlighted countries in their flag colour), characters are flag-balls with faces and noodle arms, animated as cut-out puppets _(agent)_
- **outlines**: characters and props: dark brown-black outline about 3 px at 576 wide (5 px at 1080); coastline a thin dark line (2 px at 1080) with a pale soft halo on the sea side; land borders shown only by the change of fill colour; highlighted regions have a soft darker rim _(agent)_
- **shading**: flat fills; flag-balls get a faint darker bottom-right and a soft contact shadow; no gradients on the map except the coast halo _(agent)_
- **shape language**: perfect circles for characters, wobbly noodle limbs with mitten hands, wooden plank signs on a post, real country shapes, small round icons (helmets, flags, houses) _(agent)_
- **texture**: a crumpled-paper / hillshade mottling over land and sea, low contrast (about +-4% lightness), fixed to the map _(agent)_
- **type and UI**: titles: hand-lettered rounded capitals in off-white with a dark outline and drop shadow, slightly rotated, in the top third; map labels: small letter-spaced serif capitals in dark brown; sign words: hand-lettered dark ink on wood; captions: short bold white phrases with a dark shadow near the bottom, numbers and key words amber, a character's line prefixed with a dash in its flag colour _(agent)_
- **motion style**: limited cut-out animation: balls bob, squash and blink, arms swing like puppets (held on twos), props and titles pop in with overshoot; mostly static framing per shot with slow push-ins; hard cuts between map locations; flashbacks washed out; travel shown by dotted routes and dashed arrows; one dark cinematic cutaway for the climax _(agent)_
- **signature traits**: a plank sign planted in the disputed land whose single word changes through the story; flag-balls arguing on a real map; power shown by ball size _(agent)_
- **do not copy**: the topic and every name in it (the colony, the empire, the neighbour, the ally, the alliance, the leader and the commander), all dates and numbers, the words on the signs and titles, the real archival photos in the portrait medallions, the 3D night scene; no watermark or handle is visible _(agent)_
