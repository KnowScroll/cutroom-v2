## Camera, lighting and editing

- Visual style: muted semi-realistic documentary renders and cut-aways, black-and-white evidence photos
- Framing: shot size medium close-up 63%, medium shot 31%, close-up 6%; subject is the in-focus region (no face; shot size is an estimate)
- Camera: fixed camera (tripod / mount / on the desk) 73%, moving camera (gimbal / handheld pan) 27%; typical move push-in
- Lighting: night / low-light ambient, no dedicated fill. Key light frontal (from the camera side; both sides of the subject about equally bright), at subject height; lighting ratio about 1.2:1 (0.2 stops); medium-soft; colour temperature cool (daylight / cool-white LED); background clearly blurred (shallow depth of field / portrait mode)
- (not live footage: the above describes the light in the picture, for generation prompts, not a shooting plan)
- Grade: dark and desaturated (cinematic); low saturation, normal contrast, normal blacks, overall cool
- Editing: 27.1 cuts per minute, 2.17s average shot; no live-action shots
- No pixel grid (not pixel art, or a grid too fine to measure)
- Colour: neutral, dark, 1 colour scenes
