## Camera, lighting and editing

- Visual style: Toon-shaded pixel-art 3D; dark navy interior, saturated parts, cream HUD
- Framing: shot size medium shot 50%, medium close-up 50%; subject is the in-focus region (no face; shot size is an estimate)
- Camera: fixed camera (tripod / mount / on the desk) 100%
- Lighting: soft frontal light (flat light). Key light frontal (from the camera side; both sides of the subject about equally bright), from below (screen / desk bounce / low source); lighting ratio about 1.2:1 (0.2 stops); soft light (wide lit-to-shadow ramp); colour temperature neutral (about 5000K); lower half of the subject is brighter: likely light from below (screen, desk bounce); background clearly blurred (shallow depth of field / portrait mode)

- Grade: natural (close to the original colours); normal saturation, normal contrast, normal blacks, overall cool
- Editing: 17.6 cuts per minute, 3.1s average shot; no live-action shots
- No pixel grid (not pixel art, or a grid too fine to measure)
- Colour: complementary with extra hues, dark, 1 colour scenes
