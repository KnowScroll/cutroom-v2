## Rendering (seen)

- **rendering style**: toon-shaded 3D cartoon: smooth soft shading on simple 3D forms with dark ink outlines, warm lamp-lit interiors _(agent)_
- **outlines**: dark warm-black ink outline on every object, about 4 to 5 px at 1080 wide on characters and furniture, thinner on far details _(agent)_
- **shading**: smooth gradient shading from one warm key light (top-left), lighter top faces, darker sides, soft contact shadows, vignette _(agent)_
- **shape language**: characters are a huge sphere head (about half the height) on a small tube body with stick arms ending in ball hands and stick legs ending in ball shoes; furniture and machines are simple boxes and cylinders; cone lamps _(agent)_
- **texture**: almost none: striped wallpaper, wood plank lines, panel mouldings; stubble as small dots on faces _(agent)_
- **type and UI**: bold sans text on in-world objects (screens, signs, papers, calendars) carries every number; captions one word at a time, uppercase heavy white with a black outline, centred at about 73% of the height _(agent)_
- **motion style**: snappy cartoon acting: quick expression changes (eyes popping to rings, brows jumping, sweat drops), small body moves; slow push-ins; whip pans between angles _(agent)_
- **signature traits**: the money shots are full-frame inserts of papers and screens with the key number on a yellow highlight; wall calendars mark time; reaction close-ups of the huge face _(agent)_
- **do not copy**: the topic (a junk-mail cheque deposited at a bank), the bank, cheque, ATM, safe-deposit room, the dollar figures, dates and any words on screen; the creator handle or watermark _(agent)_
