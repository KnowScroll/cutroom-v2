# Visual style

Colour is written as relations (offsets from the base hue, lightness and chroma levels by role), so it works with any base hue; the hex values at the end are the original's, for reference only: do not copy them.

## Rendering (measured)

- No pixel grid (not pixel art, or a grid too fine to measure)
- Flat fills 96%, gentle gradients 1%, hard edges 2%; dark outlines on about 23% of hard edges; no checkerboard dithering
- 1 colours cover 95% of the picture; detail score 1.91 (fastreel background-detail scale: flat gradient about 0.2, six outlined boxes 1.45); repeated frames 79% (higher = held drawings, animated on twos or lower)

## Rendering (seen)

- **rendering style**: Minimal 2D motion graphics on pure black: a glowing white figure on small diorama sets, line-art props, pill chips, a glowing data curve; one shaded 3D object near the end _(agent)_
- **outlines**: Props and devices: dark fills with 2 px pale grey outlines; slabs: no outline but a 3 px bright top-edge highlight; the figure has no outline, only a soft white bloom _(agent)_
- **shading**: Flat fills in 2 or 3 neutral greys; glow on the figure and the curve; one soft light pool per scene; no textures _(agent)_
- **shape language**: Discs, rounded bells, thin bevelled slabs, dotted arcs, measuring ticks, rounded pill chips, outlined numerals _(agent)_
- **texture**: None: clean vector, faint perspective grid on the graph plate, dashed baselines _(agent)_
- **type and UI**: Small uppercase bold pill chips (dark with a grey border for who is who; white with dark text for spoken words); a large white sentence-case caption of the full spoken line centred under the picture; outlined numerals _(agent)_
- **motion style**: Calm and slow: hops along visible dotted arcs, identical replays, chips popping in, a curve drawing itself with a counter on its tip, colour sweeping along the curve on the turn, slow camera push-ins and pull-outs _(agent)_
- **signature traits**: The same tiny failure shown to a FRIEND and to YOU, replayed side by side with two different chips; monochrome until one full-colour reveal; the reel returns to its first scene _(agent)_
- **do not copy**: The topic (self-talk after a failed jump and a failed product launch), the game controller, the platforms and jump, the desk mat, the laptop and phone UIs, the words and numbers on screen, any handle or watermark _(agent)_
